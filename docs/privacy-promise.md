# The Aura privacy promise

- **We never sell data.** There is nothing to sell: we don't keep it.
- **We never store raw ID.** Documents are deleted 60 seconds after a token
  is minted, and deletion is receipted in the audit log.
- **Apps get answers, not identities.** over18? true. That's the whole answer.
- **Revocation needs no personal data**, because keeping none is the design.
